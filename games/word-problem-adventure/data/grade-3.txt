# Hidden totals | Decide whether the story gives the starting amount or the ending amount.
wpa-g3-001|A library lends 28 books and has 47 left. How many books did it have before lending any?|75|books|28+47|The books lent and the books left make the starting total.
wpa-g3-002|Lena collects 36 leaves. This is 14 more than Hugo collects. How many leaves does Hugo collect?|22|leaves|36-14|Lena has the larger amount, so remove the extra 14.
wpa-g3-003|A shop has 85 cards. It sells some and has 39 left. How many cards does it sell?|46|cards|85-39|The difference between the starting and ending amounts is the number sold.
wpa-g3-004|Noor needs 90 points for a badge. She earns 34 points in one game and 27 in another. How many more points does she need?|29|points|90-34-27|Combine the points earned, then compare with the goal.
wpa-g3-005|A train has 64 riders after 19 riders get on. No one gets off. How many riders were on the train before the stop?|45|riders|64-19|Undo the new riders joining the train.
